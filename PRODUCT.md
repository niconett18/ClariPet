# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Pet owners looking for premium, health-conscious pet products, food, and accessories.

## Product Purpose
An e-commerce storefront for Claripet to sell products, share journal articles, and build a brand around quality pet care.

## Brand Personality
Friendly, trustworthy, modern, premium, clean.

## Anti-references
Cluttered big-box pet stores, cheap/tacky layouts, overloaded navigation.

## Design Principles
- Show, don't tell: Let product images stand out.
- Easy navigation: Shopping should be frictionless.
- Trust through design: Clean, accessible, and fast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA compliance. Full keyboard navigability, screen-reader friendly, strong contrast ratios.
