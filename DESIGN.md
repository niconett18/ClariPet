# Design

## Theme
Clean, friendly e-commerce with soft pastel accents and legible typography.

## Colors
- Primary: `#1B2A4A` (Navy)
- Background: `#FBFCFE` (Off-white)
- Accents: Sky (`#AECBEB`), Sage (`#C5D6C8`), Pink (`#F5CDD3`), Lavender (`#E0CBE8`)
- Text: `#2D3142` (Ink)

## Typography
- Family: Poppins, system-ui, sans-serif

## Components
- Cards: Soft shadows, rounded corners (18px-32px).
- Buttons: Clear hit areas, hover states.
